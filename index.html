01 // WHO AM I

A person with too many rabbit holes.

OBSERVATION SUBJECT // HUMAN
I’m Balendu, a college student with a habit of falling into subjects far larger than the syllabus intended.

I’m fascinated by history, anthropology, geography, politics, art, design, technology, and the strange ways humans organize themselves into societies.

Sometimes that curiosity turns into a painting. Sometimes a presentation, a website, a hackathon project, a page of notes, or an unnecessarily deep conversation about civilization.

I like learning across boundaries because the most interesting ideas rarely stay inside one subject.
